## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am a **Statistician, Data Analyst, Machine Learning Researcher, and university educator** working at the intersection of statistical modeling, artificial intelligence, public health, and climate-related risk.

I hold an **MSc in Applied Statistics** and am a **PhD candidate in Applied Statistics at Chuka University, Kenya**, where my research interests focus on developing hybrid statistical and machine-learning approaches for complex, high-dimensional and spatiotemporal problems.

I am also the **Founder of Beyond Data Analytics**, an initiative focused on transforming data into actionable insights through statistical analysis, machine learning, explainable AI, research consulting, and data science capacity building.

My broader goal is to contribute to the development of **transparent, reproducible, and context-aware data science solutions for African settings**, particularly where decisions have direct implications for health, livelihoods, and climate resilience.

# 🚀 Featured Projects

## ❤️ CardioPredict AI

**Explainable AI for cardiovascular disease risk prediction**

A machine-learning application developed around the Cleveland heart-disease dataset, combining multiple classification algorithms with explainability techniques.

**Models evaluated:**

`Logistic Regression` · `Random Forest` · `Extra Trees` · `XGBoost` · `Gradient Boosting` · `AdaBoost` · `KNN` · `SVM` · `Naive Bayes`

The project emphasizes **recall and false-negative reduction**, reflecting the importance of identifying patients who may be at elevated risk.

### Key components

* Stratified train/test evaluation
* Cross-validation
* Hyperparameter optimization
* ROC-AUC and PR-AUC
* Confusion-matrix analysis
* SHAP-based explanations
* Individual risk interpretation
* Risk stratification
* Interactive Streamlit deployment

🔗 **Live application:**
https://ai-driven-prediction-of-heart-disease-cdam.streamlit.app/

# 📈 What I Care About

My work is guided by five principles:

**01 — Statistical Rigor**
Models should be grounded in sound statistical methodology.

**02 — Interpretability**
Important decisions should not depend entirely on black-box predictions.

**03 — Reproducibility**
Research should be documented, version-controlled, and independently reproducible.

**04 — Context**
Methods developed in one setting should not automatically be assumed to work equally well in another.

**05 — Impact**
The ultimate purpose of data science is not simply prediction — it is better decision-making.
